## Client edits (28 Aug 2026)

Approved by the owner in the second review, so these now **override** the
straight translation above. Kept here because they are departures from the
brochure and someone will eventually ask why.

| Where | Was | Now | His reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero standfirst | "coordinating **everything** it takes to place…" | "coordinating **many of the steps** it takes to place…" | *"(Not everything)"* — the brochure overclaims the scope of what Bizzners runs |
| Ally heading | "A negotiation ally with broad vision" | "…broad vision, **in solid and new markets**" | wanted the reach named in the heading |
| Ally ¶1 | "participation in **solid** markets" | "participation in **solid and new** markets" | same, consistently |
| Ally ¶2 | "a **versatile, multidisciplinary** unit of collaborators" | "a **talented and well-rounded** unit of collaborators" | his own phrasing |
| Hero kicker | "International trade facilitation · Panamá" | "International trade facilitation" | *"podemos eliminar la palabra Panamá"* — the offer is international, not local |
| Hero chip | "Panamá → global markets" | "Global markets" | same |

"Panamá" is deliberately **kept** in the contact block and the legal line: it is
where the company is, and the trademark line is a legal statement.

## Deliberate choices

- **No invented numbers** (clients served, countries, success rates). The only
  numerals shown are structural (7 pillars, 8 moves, 5 tools).
- Email normalized to lowercase `javier.lopez@bizzners.com`.
- "Panamá" keeps its accent in English copy as a brand-flavor choice — happy to
  flatten to "Panama" if preferred.
